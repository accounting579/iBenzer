## Lessons
- Read General and Special Conditions.
- Complete signed certifications beyond the price sheet.
- Manufacturer list-price support may be mandatory for discount-based bids.
- Alternate products may require clearly marked descriptive literature.
- Mailing labels, physical delivery rules, and electronic spreadsheet copies may all be separate requirements.
- Never copy historical discount percentages or prices without current confirmation.
