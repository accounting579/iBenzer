# iBenzer Bid Rules

Extracted from the internal VA bid SOP and current tracker conventions.

## Triage
- Prioritize bids expiring soon.
- Record Directly Relevant and Indirectly Relevant bids in tracker/CRM.
- Do not clutter the list with clearly Not Relevant work.
- Historical SOP note: quantities no greater than 100 were often deleted without further consideration. For borderline/current user-selected bids, flag this for confirmation rather than silently discarding.
- United Nations opportunities are marked in the SOP as automatic delete.
- Contracts usually have no fixed quantity; determine participation viability.
- Contract/cooperative result timing differs from ordinary line-item bids.

## Review
Read the entire solicitation. Mandatory forms/signature locations may be outside obvious response forms.

Focus on Specification, Scope of Work, Proposal/Bid Form, certifications, exclusions/deviations, addenda, and required attachments.

## No blanks
Required forms should not be left blank. If a required answer is not supported by a reliable source, ask the bid contact or escalate internally. Never invent an answer.

## Product/manufacturer
- For alternates/equivalents, provide manufacturer, brand/model/catalog information and sufficient literature.
- A reseller does not certify the manufacturer’s product; use manufacturer-issued certifications.
- Keep product proposal/specification, warranty, flyer/catalog, drop-test certificate, CA65, and similar support material available when relevant.

## Mail/sample timing
For mailed bids or samples, target shipment at least five business days before due date. Obtain pricing early enough to support that schedule.

## Result follow-up
- Ordinary bid: first inquiry a little over one week after submission; if pending, ask expected timing and then follow weekly.
- Contract/cooperative: begin result inquiry after about one month unless the solicitation says otherwise.
- Record award/tabulation details and competitor pricing when available.

## Approval boundary
Explicit approval is required before formal bid submission, external email/message, signature, binding price/commitment, purchase/order, or other irreversible external action.
